MultimodalPhysioKit was developed and is maintained by Dr. Gabriele Luzzani
as research software for multimodal physiological signal processing.

The MATLAB and Python source code in MultimodalPhysioKit was independently
authored for this project. Cited publications are methodological and
scientific references, not source-code origins. MultimodalPhysioKit v0.1.0
bundles no third-party source code; external dependencies retain their own
licenses.

The signal-processing and feature-extraction routines are based on methods
published by Luzzani and collaborators in the following peer-reviewed papers,
which are listed in CITATION.cff:

- Luzzani, G., Buraioli, I., Guglieri, G., and Demarchi, D. (2025).
  "EDA, PPG and Skin Temperature as Predictive Signals for Mental Failure by
  a Statistical Analysis on Stress and Mental Workload." IEEE Open Journal of
  Engineering in Medicine and Biology, 6, 248-255.
  https://doi.org/10.1109/OJEMB.2024.3515473

- Luzzani, G., Pogliano, M., Buraioli, I., Colavincenzo, M., Martorana, S.,
  Guglieri, G., and Demarchi, D. (2025). "ECG, Respiration, fNIRS, and Eye
  Tracking for Stress and Mental Workload Monitoring in Human-Machine
  Interaction." IEEE Access, 13, 122726-122741.
  https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3588384

Raw datasets and acquisition records used in those publications are not
distributed with this software.

Users of this repository in academic work should cite the software and the
relevant methodological publication(s) for the modalities they use.

The EDA workflow depends on cvxEDA. cvxEDA is external software, is not
bundled with this repository, and remains subject to its own GNU GPL license
and citation requirements.
